When the sun sets below the horizon and darkness begins to cover the Snowy Valley Village, the lights in each house slowly turn off. Children tuck themselves into thick blankets. But lately, many children in the village have been having trouble sleeping. They often wake up in the middle of the night because of nightmares about scary dark shadows.

Grandpa **"Elias"** was a kind old man with a long beard as white as snow. He always wore a blue sleeping cap with stars and a cozy blue pajama set that looked warm. Grandpa Elias wasn't an ordinary villager; he was the last "Dream Weaver" of the village.

Every night, Grandpa Elias would carry his "Magic Lantern" and walk along the quiet streets. His lantern wasn't lit by fire, but it emitted a soft, swirling golden light inside. That light was magic that collected "sweet memories" lingering in the air throughout the day, such as the smell of fresh baked bread, the sound of laughter while playing, or a lullaby sung by a mother.

"Tonight, nightmares are bothering the children again," Grandpa Elias murmured to himself as he saw a small dark shadow hovering over a boy's house. He raised his magic lantern. The golden light from the lantern slowly absorbed those dark shadows until they disappeared, replacing them with warmth.

After walking around collecting sweet memories until his lantern was full, Grandpa Elias returned to his small wooden cabin, which was his dream-weaving workshop.

![Middle Image: Elias spinning glowing golden thread in his workshop](/images/dream-weaver-middle.jpg)

Inside the cabin, glowing golden threads were floating everywhere. Grandpa Elias sat down at his magical spinning wheel. He took the light from his lantern and spun it into the softest thread in the world. Each thread was filled with stories of happiness and fun adventures.

"This thread is for a dream about a glowing flower field," Grandpa said as he spun the thread. "And this thread is for a dream about riding a kind dragon flying in the sky."

Grandpa's wrinkled but loving hands slowly wove those threads together into a "Dream Blanket" that was as light as a cloud but warmed you to the core of your heart. Each blanket sparkled as if tiny stars were hidden inside.

When the dream blankets were finished, Grandpa Elias set out again. He tiptoed into the bedrooms of children who were restless from nightmares.

![End Image: Elias tucking a sleeping child in with a dream blanket](/images/dream-weaver-end.jpg)

Grandpa walked to the bed of a little boy whose eyebrows were tightly furrowed. He gently draped the dream blanket, woven with starlight, over the little boy. As soon as the blanket touched his body, a gentle golden light spread throughout the room, driving away all the darkness and nightmares until they completely vanished.

The little boy's furrowed brows slowly relaxed, and a faint, happy smile appeared on his lips. He was no longer dreaming of scary dark shadows; he was dreaming of running in the most beautiful rainbow flower field.

Grandpa Elias did this for every child in the village until everyone was fast asleep with smiles on their faces. Then he walked back to his cabin with a heart full of joy.

"Good night, children. Sweet dreams," Grandpa Elias whispered softly to the wind.

The moon in the sky shone brightly, as if smiling in gratitude to the kind Dream Weaver. Tonight would be another peaceful night in the Snowy Valley Village, filled with the sweetest dreams. And when tomorrow morning came, the children would wake up full of energy and brightness to create new sweet memories for Grandpa Elias to weave into dreams the following night.

Close your eyes now, good child. Don't be afraid of the dark, because in your dreams, there will always be light from Grandpa Elias's magic lantern to protect you and keep you warm... Good night.

---

**Moral of the Story:** Even in the darkest times, there is always the warmth and light of hope protecting us. Just close your eyes with a peaceful heart and let go, and sweet dreams will come to you.
